Q4. Describe the most likely migration approach that will suit the chosen company.
A.  This project adopts a hybrid cloud approach, migrating only the CRM system to the cloud while keeping other departmental software on-premises. This strategy enables rapid global coverage to support international sales, reduces personnel costs and simplifies sales processes, improves sales and production efficiency, and lowers change costs, especially by reducing the IT workload.

Q5. Produce a high level schedule for the migration process
A.  This case actually took one year and four months.we need to plan from the following perspectives: business perspective, platform perspective, and operational perspective to make this scedule as following: 
1. Business Perspective
Goal: Ensure alignment with business objectives, minimize disruption, and enhance global sales operations.
Timeline：Month 1-4 Week 1–4: Define business objectives and key performance indicators (KPIs) for migration.
Week 5–8: Identify stakeholders and establish a communication plan.
Week 9–12: Conduct impact analysis on sales and other related departments.
2. Platform Perspective
Goal: Migrate the CRM system to the cloud while maintaining integration with on-premises systems.
Timeline: Month 4–6
Week 13–16: Assess the existing infrastructure and select the cloud platform for CRM.
Week 17–20: Design the architecture, including integration points with on-premises systems.
Week 21–24: Set up the cloud environment and conduct a test migration with sample data.
3. Operational Perspective
Goal: Ensure a smooth transition with minimal operational disruption and optimized IT workloads.
Timeline: Month 7–9
Week 25–28: Train sales and IT teams on the new CRM system.
Week 29–32: Perform full data migration and system validation.
Week 33–36: Monitor post-migration performance, collect feedback, and optimize workflows.
